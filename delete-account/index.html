Delete your Mochi account

In the app

  1. Open Mochi and tap the Settings star.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is removed from the app right away.

This cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the app

Email support@mochitodo.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask a question or two to check it's yours. We delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your tasks and your Backlog
  • Shared tasks and love notes you created
  • Your partner link (your partner is unlinked)
  • Your settings, hearts, Mochi Bucks and unlocked skins
  • Your invite code, referral records and redeemed codes

What is kept

  • Things your partner wrote. They stay in your partner's account.
  • A log that a link or unlink happened, with your identity removed.
  • Copies in routine backups, which are cleared on their normal schedule soon after.
  • Apple's own records of your purchases, which Apple keeps under its privacy policy.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not cancel Mochi Pro. Apple handles the billing, so cancel on your iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.
