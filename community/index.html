Community Rules

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Mochi is for doing your todos, alone or with one person you link with. Love notes, shared tasks and task suggestions only go to that person. These rules keep it a kind place. They are part of our Terms.

What is not allowed

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior. Do not send or store anything that:

  • threatens, harasses, bullies, stalks, or pressures someone;
  • is hateful toward people because of their race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, or where they are from;
  • is sexual content involving anyone under 18;
  • is sexual or graphic content sent to someone who does not want it;
  • encourages self-harm, suicide, or violence;
  • is illegal, or shares someone's private information without their permission;
  • is spam, a scam, or an attempt to trick someone.

Do not link with someone to harass them, and do not make fake accounts.

How we keep it safe

  • Filtering. When you use the AI "Improve" feature, a safety filter checks your text before and after it's rewritten and blocks anything harmful.
  • Reporting. You can report anything your partner made from inside the app: open a love note and tap Report, swipe a shared task left and tap the flag, or tap Report under a task suggestion. You can also email us.
  • Blocking. In Settings, tap Block Partner. This unlinks you both right away. They can no longer send you notes, tasks, or suggestions.
  • Our response. We aim to review every report within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these rules and may suspend or close the account that sent it.

If you are in danger

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first. Then block the person and tell us.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question: support@mochitodo.com.
